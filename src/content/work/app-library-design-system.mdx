platform:
  - My O2 App
  - My VM App
  - Priority App
outcome: Theme application cut from 54+ seconds to under 4 seconds, with one shared foundation across three brands that now feeds into the wider Creative Design System (CDS).
brief: "Teams were recreating similar components across three brands, creating inconsistent states and design-to-development drift. I needed to help shape a cross-brand system that balanced consistency with flexibility and still supported live journeys and legacy components. It also meant moving designers from Tokens Studio to native Figma Variables."

<CaseSection number="04" title="Outcome and next steps">
  <p>With variable-driven components, applying a brand theme to a design went from 54+ seconds to under 4 seconds. Across 7,609 recorded component inserts, all three app teams now build from the same shared library instead of local copies.</p>
  <p>Next, learnings from the App Library will feed into the wider CDS, shaping shared variable structures, component patterns and governance across Virgin Media and O2.</p>
