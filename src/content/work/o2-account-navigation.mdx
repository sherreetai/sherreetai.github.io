challengeHeading: No single source of truth existed for the app’s information architecture or billing hierarchy.
challenge: Customers faced unnecessary friction when switching between products and accounts. My role was to design a more intuitive, product-led navigation experience while working within the existing account and billing structure.
outcome: Halved product re-selection from 6 steps to 3 in the redesigned tariff-change and Bolt-On journeys.
---

<CaseSection eyebrow="Understanding the complexity" title="Built the missing system map" media>
  <p>I brought together billing, backend engineering, frontline retail and eCare to understand how accounts, products and billing worked, then consolidated the findings into a shared IA and billing logic map.</p>
  <p>The map captured different rules for finance agreements, companion devices and SIM Free Finance, rather than treating everything as one simple product hierarchy.</p>
</CaseSection>

<CaseSection eyebrow="Changing the mental model" title="Shifted navigation from accounts to products">
  <p>I redesigned the experience around the product customers were actively managing, while keeping billing information at account level. This brought the app closer to the product-led web experience and reduced the need to switch between accounts.</p>
</CaseSection>

<CaseSection eyebrow="Changing the mental model" title="Simplified the product view" media>
  <p>I replaced the carousel with a static product view and removed unnecessary hierarchy and headings, making the active product and key actions easier to find.</p>
</CaseSection>

<CaseSection eyebrow="Changing the mental model" title="Removed re-selection">
  <p>I challenged journeys where customers selected a product, then had to select it again. Actions such as Buy Bolt-On could retain the selected product context and take customers directly to the relevant journey.</p>
</CaseSection>

<CaseSection eyebrow="A simple path through the account" title="Reduced re-selection from 6 steps to 3">
  <p>The redesigned multi-product journey reduced re-selection from 6 steps to 3 across tariff-change and Bolt-On flows. Carrying product context through the journey removed unnecessary steps and created a simpler, more consistent experience.</p>
</CaseSection>
