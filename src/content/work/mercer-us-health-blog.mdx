  Mercer, a global consulting firm, had a US Health Blog with a discoverability problem. Most visitors arrived via search or external links, landing directly on articles with little context about where they were or how to explore further. The blog lacked clear structure, making it hard for users to find related content or understand the broader site.
challenge: Redesign the blog to improve navigation and content discovery for users arriving mid-site without breaking from Mercer's established global design system.
---

## Design Decisions

### Landing page

Designed a flexible featured content section giving editors the choice to manually spotlight an article or let the three most recent populate automatically. The hero included accessible overlay options using fills from Mercer's gradient palette, ensuring visual consistency and accessibility.

<Media src="/images/mercer/landing.svg" alt="US Health News landing page" />

<PullQuote>Users landing deep in the site gained immediate context and clear paths to explore further.</PullQuote>

### Navigation

Introduced a sticky secondary nav bar to help users understand where they were within the site. A persistent search bar and topic filters let visitors quickly find relevant content, regardless of where they entered.

**1** – A sticky secondary nav sits below the page header, keeping orientation and key actions accessible as users scroll.

**2** – Tapping the search icon expands a full search bar inline, letting users query content without leaving the page.

**3** – Tapping a topic tag opens a drawer with a full list of topic links, making category browsing quick and discoverable.

**4** – Desktop view of the secondary nav below the landing page hero, showing how featured content and navigation coexist at full width.

<Media src="/images/mercer/nav-mobile.svg" alt="Mobile secondary navigation" layout="panel" />

<Media src="/images/mercer/nav-desktop.svg" alt="Desktop secondary navigation" layout="panel" />

### Newsletter

Replaced intrusive pop-ups with an inline subscription form embedded within article pages, allowing users to sign up within the natural flow of reading.

<Media src="/images/mercer/newsletter.svg" alt="Inline newsletter sign-up" layout="panel" />

## Outcome

The redesign improved clarity and discoverability, giving users better context when landing deep in the site. Authors and editors gained flexibility to highlight content without extra maintenance and the blog maintained alignment with Mercer's global brand system.
